The brief

Our team proposed mixed-tenure housing, a community recreation hub, local services and connected green space for Randwick Defence Land. The challenge was to bring the community vision together with a credible approach to implementation.

My contribution

I led the development of the proposal, taking primary responsibility for project direction, task delegation and pitch preparation. My presentation work focused on implementation stages, key risks, measured outcomes and the recommendation.

Decisions that shaped the work

01

Stage the delivery

Developed a 10–12-year implementation proposal covering approvals, site preparation and staged construction. The programme included a two-year buffer for delays.

02

Measurable targets

Defined five performance targets for the proposal, including 70% social or affordable housing, 100% renewable electricity and 90% construction waste reuse or recycling. These were targets for the proposed precinct.

03

Plan for environmental constraints

Included controls for emissions performance and protection of native habitats, rather than treating these as separate issues at the end of the proposal.
